A quiet Sunday walk

Good Calm Grateful

Took the long way home by the river. The light did that soft orange thing and I stopped to watch the rowers for a while.

A dog on the bridge refused to move until everyone said hello. Everyone did.

Note to self: phone in the bag, not in the hand. The walk felt twice as long, in a good way.
